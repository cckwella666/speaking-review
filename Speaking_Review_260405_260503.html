Grammar Speaking Drill
Speak first, then write
Study the grammar pattern, then complete each sentence ALOUD before you write. Click "Hint" if you need help.
Pattern A
used to + verb  /  didn't use to + verb
  • 1
    I used to , but now I don't anymore.
    Hint
    Think of an old habit you've given up. (e.g., staying up late, eating fast food, etc.)
  • 2
    I didn't use to enjoy , but I've changed my mind because…
    Hint
    Think of something you disliked but now appreciate. (e.g., a type of food, a subject, exercise…)
  • 3
    My used to be very different when I was younger.
    Hint
    Fill in: routine / attitude / goals / lifestyle. Then explain how it's changed!
Pattern B
wish + past simple  (expressing a wish about the present)
  • 4
    I wish I more time to focus on things that matter.
    Hint
    Use: had / spent / didn't waste. Remember: "wish + past simple" for present situations!
  • 5
    I sometimes wish I , because…
    Hint
    e.g., "I wish I lived abroad / could speak another language / were more patient…" Add a reason!
Pattern C
It takes / It costs / It spends — choosing correctly
  • 6
    Building a good habit (takes / costs / spends) a lot of consistency and patience.
    Hint
    "It takes time/effort" — use TAKES for time and effort. COSTS = money/sacrifice. SPEND = subject spends time/money.
  • 7
    Changing careers (takes / costs / spends) a lot of courage, and it can also you financially.
    Hint
    First blank: "takes courage." Second blank: "cost you" (financial sacrifice).

Spot the Error
Find it · Fix it · Say it right!
Each sentence has ONE mistake. Say the correct version aloud, then click "Reveal" to check. Can you explain WHY it's wrong?
  • She used to working late every night when she was in university.
    ✅ She used to work late every night when she was in university.
    Why: "used to" is followed by the base verb (infinitive), not "-ing".
  • It spends a lot of time to learn a new language fluently.
    ✅ It takes a lot of time to learn a new language fluently.
    Why: "It takes time/effort" is the correct structure. "Spend" needs a subject (e.g., "She spends a lot of time…").
  • I wish I have more discipline when it comes to exercise.
    ✅ I wish I had more discipline when it comes to exercise.
    Why: "wish" about the present uses past simple. "I wish I had…" (not "have").
  • My attitude to failure didn't used to be so positive — I've grown a lot.
    ✅ My attitude to failure didn't use to be so positive — I've grown a lot.
    Why: In negatives, we say "didn't use to" (no extra 'd'). The 'd' is already in "didn't".
  • Making a big life change costs a lot of courage and mental energy.
    ✅ Making a big life change takes a lot of courage and mental energy.
    Why: "costs" is for financial sacrifices or abstract loss. For time/effort/courage, use "takes".

Discussion Questions
Answer in 3–5 sentences
Answer each question aloud in 3–5 sentences. Try to use vocabulary from this unit. You can also write key words in the lines provided.
  • 1
    Think of one habit you've tried to build or break. Did consistency help, or did you give up? What would you do differently now?
    Try to use: used to, consistency, discipline, setback, intentional
  • 2
    Some people say: "Don't hesitate — just do it." Others say: "Think carefully before you act." Which advice do you follow more? Give an example.
    Try to use: hesitate, trade-off, consequence, perspective, I wish I had…
  • 3
    Do you think people can truly change their personalities, or do we mostly stay the same? Share your view with examples from real life.
    Try to use: used to, adjust to, overcome, perspective, I didn't use to think that…
  • 4
    Bonus: Describe a time in your life when everything felt uncertain. How did you adjust? What helped you the most?
    This is open — speak freely and try to use at least 3 words from today's vocabulary!
